title: Configure an agent team

Use a team when a design question would benefit from a geometry review and an independent modeling plan.

1. In the Assistant, choose the **Agent** and **Model** for the lead. Enable **Team** and open **Team settings**.
2. Configure the two roles. The **Geometry reviewer** checks the geometry and identifies what needs verification. The **Modeling planner** proposes operations and their order.
3. Leave **Same as lead** selected to reuse the lead's settings, or choose a different agent, model, and supported effort for a role. Choose **Save team**.
4. Send your request. Follow each role's updates in the conversation. The workers review the supplied context in parallel. The lead receives their findings and can request changes to the live model in sequence when **Tools: Live** is enabled.

The approval mode you choose for the lead still applies to Studio edits. See [Agent roles and skills](roles-and-skills.html) for the role briefs and available workflows.
