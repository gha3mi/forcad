title: Export a patch as IGES
category: Projects and exchange

1. Select one curve or surface patch under **Scene → Geometry**. IGES export is available for these two patch ranks.
2. Choose **Project → Export IGES**. Choose the destination and enter a filename ending in `.igs` or `.iges`.
3. After Studio reports the export path, open the file in the receiving CAD application to inspect the curve or surface.

**Fortran equivalent.** [Export a NURBS circle to IGES](../fortran/export-curve-iges.html) and check its entity type and units.
