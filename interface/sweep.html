sweep_curve sweep_surface

public interface sweep

Construct a fixed-frame translational sweep along a NURBS curve.

The first argument is a curve or surface profile and the second argument is the spine curve. Source knot vectors, degrees, and wrapping policies are retained in their corresponding output directions.

private pure function sweep_surface(profile, spine, origin) result(volume)

Author
Seyed Ali Ghasemi
License
BSD 3-Clause

Sweep a NURBS surface by exact translation along a NURBS spine. The profile orientation remains fixed. The optional origin is subtracted from the spine before translation. The surface directions become output directions 1 and 2; the spine becomes direction 3.
