public pure subroutine tensor_basis_derivatives2_local(first1, nc1, ders1, first2, nc2, ders2, first3, nc3, ders3, basis, gradient, hessian, Wc)

Compute a tensor-product basis, gradient, and Hessian on local support. The derivative tables contain rows zero through two. Unused directions use a three-row, one-column identity table. Rational derivatives apply the quotient rule through second total order. This is a fused tensor-product specialization of the multivariate quotient recurrence, extending the rational-surface recurrence in Piegl and Tiller, The NURBS Book, Algorithm A4.4 to as many as three parametric directions. Direction 1 varies fastest in basis. gradient(:,i) stores , while hessian(:,i,j) stores the mixed derivative .
