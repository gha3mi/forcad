private pure subroutine set_C(this, center, radius)

Construct an exact planar rational C-shaped circular arc.

The open curve consists of two quadratic rational arcs spanning 240 degrees. It is embedded in the xy plane at center(3). Its geometric radius is abs(radius); a negative value rotates the template by , while zero collapses it to the center.
