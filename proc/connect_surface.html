private pure subroutine connect_surface(this, patch_a, side_a, patch_b, side_b, continuity, reverse, geometric, reparameterization)

Record two surface edges with parametric or restricted geometric metadata. geometric defaults to .false. for C^n continuity. For G^n, reparameterization stores the arbitrary-order transition jet between normalized coordinates normal to the connected sides. Tangential orientation is controlled independently by reverse. The geometric map is restricted to ; no transition derivative may vary along the edge or couple normal motion into the tangential coordinate.

The connected boundary curves must have equal control counts, equal tangential degree, and affine-equivalent normalized knot vectors. Their physical traces, derivative residuals and, for rational patches, projective weight scales are checked by is_valid. Call that method before assembly or analysis; successful connect only establishes valid metadata and trace space shape.
