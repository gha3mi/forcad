private pure subroutine ansatz(this, ie, ig, Tgc, dTgc_dXg, dA, ngauss, d2Tgc_dXg2)

Compute element-local IGA basis data at one quadrature point.

Element and local basis ordering agree with cmp_elem. dA includes the tensor Gauss weight, span maps, and either the absolute planar Jacobian or for an embedded surface metric . For a planar two-coordinate mapping, gradients are physical Cartesian gradients. For an embedded surface, they are ambient Cartesian components of the tangential gradient; optional Hessians are intrinsic covariant surface Hessians represented as ambient tangential tensors. A singular Jacobian or rank-deficient metric sets diagnostic code 108.
