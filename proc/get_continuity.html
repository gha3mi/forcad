private pure function get_continuity(this, dir) result(c)

Return at every distinct stored directional knot.

For degree and knot multiplicity , entry is . At interior active knots this is guaranteed spline-space continuity; a particular volume mapping can be smoother. Endpoint entries and entries outside the active interval describe representation multiplicity, not continuity between two active spans.
