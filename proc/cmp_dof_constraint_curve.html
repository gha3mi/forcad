private pure subroutine cmp_dof_constraint_curve(this, rowptr, col, val, geometric)

Assemble sparse and interface constraints. When geometric is present, only connections of that kind are included. rows use the complete arbitrary-order Faa di Bruno chain rule for the stored normalized normal reparameterization jet.

The returned arrays encode a one-based compressed sparse row matrix : nonzeros of row i occupy rowptr(i):rowptr(i+1)-1, and continuity is imposed as . Columns use unmerged patch-concatenated numbering. An absent filter includes both and connections. Apply the raw matrix to polynomial B-spline coefficients or to each homogeneous geometry column. For a rational scalar field with control coefficients , apply it to (equivalently, use ); applying directly to the unweighted coefficients is generally not sufficient.

In normalized boundary coordinates the row for derivative order represents

Parameter-range powers convert derivatives of the stored knot coordinates to normalized derivatives. Compatible rational traces also include their single projective boundary-weight scale. Call is_valid first; this assembly routine assumes compatible recorded connections. Coefficients with absolute value at most 128*epsilon(rk) are omitted from the returned CSR storage. The result is therefore a numerically sparsified floating-point matrix, not a symbolic exact matrix.
