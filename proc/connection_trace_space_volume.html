private pure function connection_trace_space_volume(this, conn, tol) result(ok)

Test whether connected faces have compatible tangential spline spaces.

After applying swap and flip metadata, both tangential traces must have equal degrees, equal control counts, and affine-equivalent normalized knot vectors.
