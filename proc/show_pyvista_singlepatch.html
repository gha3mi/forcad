public impure subroutine show_pyvista_singlepatch(vtkfile_Xc, vtkfile_Xg, vtkfile_Xth_in_Xg, rank_name)

Display previously exported single-patch VTK files with PyVista.

This routine does not export geometry. It starts a synchronous Python process and returns after the interactive window closes. Defining NOSHOW_PYVISTA at compilation makes the routine a no-op.
