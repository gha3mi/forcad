private impure subroutine show(this, vtkfile_Xc, vtkfile_Xg, vtkfile_Xth_in_Xg)

Display previously exported curve representations with PyVista.

The viewer discovers scalar point fields stored in the sampled Xg file and exposes field and colormap selectors in its window. This procedure never exports files.
