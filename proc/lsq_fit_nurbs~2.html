Alternately fit surface control points and positive rational weights.

Control points and log-weights are updated from banded normal equations. Mean log-weight removal fixes projective scale at geometric mean weight one. In exact arithmetic exponentiation preserves strict positivity; machine overflow or underflow is not checked. Damping is reduced geometrically, without a trial-step acceptance test or line search.

With and residuals , the monitored quantity is

This is not an RMS residual. Iteration stops when , so an increase also terminates a run with nonnegative tol. Reaching maxit is not an error; the last iterate is retained. Only controls, weights, and rational-state metadata are updated; cached Xg is not recomputed. Inputs must provide enough finite rows and at least two parameter columns and one coordinate column, with ndata>=nc componentwise, maxit>0, tol>=0, and nonnegative regularization/damping values. These preconditions are not all validated, and the objective is nonconvex.

Damped least-squares references: K. Levenberg, "A Method for the Solution of Certain Non-Linear Problems in Least Squares," Quarterly of Applied Mathematics 2 (1944), 164--168 (doi:10.1090/qam/10666); D. W. Marquardt, "An Algorithm for Least-Squares Estimation of Nonlinear Parameters," Journal of the Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics 11 (1963), 431--441 (doi:10.1137/0111030).
