private pure subroutine put_to_nurbs(this, X, elemConn)

Parameterize rows by their first coordinate and cache the mapped curve.

The first column of X is affinely mapped from its data range to the active knot interval :

Other columns of X do not affect the parameterization. The routine replaces the cached geometry points with ; optional connectivity replaces only their visualization connectivity.
