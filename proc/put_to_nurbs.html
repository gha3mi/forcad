private pure subroutine put_to_nurbs(this, X, elemConn)

Parameterize rows by three coordinates and cache the mapped volume.

Columns 1--3 of X are independently mapped from their data ranges to the three active knot intervals. For direction ,

Remaining columns do not affect the parameterization. The cache receives one physical volume point per input row.
