private pure subroutine set_tetragon(this, L, nc, Wc)

Construct an axis-aligned rectangular tensor-product surface.

The control net is uniformly distributed on [0,L(1)] x [0,L(2)] in the plane z=0; direction 1 varies fastest. The generated knot vectors follow the standard set(nc=...,Xc=...) policy.
