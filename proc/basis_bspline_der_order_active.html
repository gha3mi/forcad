public pure subroutine basis_bspline_der_order_active(Xt, knot, nc, degree, nder, first, B)

Evaluate one arbitrary derivative order on local support only.

The fixed-size result B(0:degree) avoids a dense temporary. first maps B(j) to global basis index first+j.
