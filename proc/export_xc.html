private impure subroutine export_Xc(this, filename, point_data, field_names, encoding)

Export the control lattice as legacy VTK hexahedral cells.

Stored visualization connectivity is used when available; otherwise trilinear connectivity is generated. Optional point_data is point-associated data with matching field_names.
