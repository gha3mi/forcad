private pure subroutine cmp_elemConn_Cn_S(nnode1, nnode2, p1, p2, Xth1, Xth2, vecKnot_mul1, vecKnot_mul2, elemConn)

Build surface connectivity as the tensor product of two span maps. Rows and local controls both use direction-1-fastest ordering. The knot values are not read; each directional size supplies its element count. Directional control counts and multiplicities must satisfy the scalar consistency relation documented by elemConn_Cn.
