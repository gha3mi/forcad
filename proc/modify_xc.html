private pure subroutine modify_Xc(this, X, num, dir)

Replace one coordinate of one flattened control point.

num is the one-based direction-1-fastest control index and dir selects the physical coordinate. X is expected to be finite but is not checked. Cached geometry is not recomputed; call create when samples must follow the modified control lattice.
