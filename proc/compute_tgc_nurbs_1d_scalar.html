private pure function compute_Tgc_nurbs_1d_scalar(Xt, knot, degree, nc, Wc, elem, wrap_parameters) result(Tgc)

Evaluate the rational curve basis at one parameter.

Without elem, the result is dense with length nc. With elem, its order follows the supplied global control indices; Wc may be global or element-local. Invalid and inactive listed indices remain zero.
