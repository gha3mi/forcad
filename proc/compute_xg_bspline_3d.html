private pure function compute_Xg_bspline_3d(Xt, knot1, knot2, knot3, degree, nc, ng, Xc, wrap_parameters) result(Xg)

Evaluate a polynomial B-spline volume at a list of parameter triples.

Evaluation accumulates only (degree(1)+1)*(degree(2)+1)*(degree(3)+1) local controls per point and avoids constructing a dense basis matrix.
