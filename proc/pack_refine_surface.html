private pure subroutine pack_refine_surface(Xc, Wc, nc, dir, rational, Xdir)

Pack surface control lines for one-dimensional refinement.

The selected direction becomes the first dimension of Xdir; all orthogonal lines and physical coordinates are packed into its columns. Rational input is converted to homogeneous form by storing followed by one weight column per orthogonal control line. dir must be 1 or 2 and Wc must be present when rational is true.
