private pure subroutine set_half_ring(this, center, radius1, radius2)

Construct an exact planar rational half-annulus.

Two quadratic quarter-arcs form each semicircular boundary and a linear radial direction connects them. The result has a 5 x 2 control net in the xy plane at center(3). Use nonzero scales of the same sign and unequal absolute values for a regular half-annulus. A negative common sign rotates the template by ; opposite signs produce crossing radial lines.
