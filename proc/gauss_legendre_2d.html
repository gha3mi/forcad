private pure subroutine gauss_legendre_2D(interval1, interval2, degree, Xksi, Wksi)

Generate a tensor-product Gauss-Legendre rule on a rectangle.

Direction 1 varies fastest. Xksi has shape [(degree(1)+1)*(degree(2)+1),2]; Wksi contains the corresponding products of one-dimensional weights. degree must contain at least two nonnegative entries, and both interval arrays must contain at least two finite entries with increasing first pair; these preconditions are not shape-validated by this wrapper.
