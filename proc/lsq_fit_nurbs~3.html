Alternately fit NURBS control points and positive rational weights.

For fixed weights, control coordinates are solved from banded normal equations. Log-weights are then updated by a damped Gauss-Newton normal equation. Subtracting their mean fixes the projective scale at geometric mean weight one. In exact arithmetic exponentiation preserves strict positivity; overflow or underflow of the machine exponential is not checked. The damping is reduced geometrically and no trial-step acceptance or line search is performed.

With residuals , the monitored quantity is

which is a scaled Euclidean norm, not the root-mean-square residual. Iteration stops when ; an objective increase therefore also stops a run with nonnegative tol. Reaching maxit is not reported as an error, and the last iterate is retained. Only Xc, Wc, and rational-state metadata are updated; cached Xg is not recomputed.

Warning

This nonlinear problem is nonconvex and uses normal equations; the result depends on the initial weights and may be a local fit. Inputs must provide at least ndata finite parameter/data rows and at least one coordinate, with ndata>=nc, maxit>0, tol>=0, and nonnegative regularization/damping values. These preconditions are not all validated.

Damped least-squares references: K. Levenberg, "A Method for the Solution of Certain Non-Linear Problems in Least Squares," Quarterly of Applied Mathematics 2 (1944), 164--168 (doi:10.1090/qam/10666); D. W. Marquardt, "An Algorithm for Least-Squares Estimation of Nonlinear Parameters," Journal of the Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics 11 (1963), 431--441 (doi:10.1137/0111030).
