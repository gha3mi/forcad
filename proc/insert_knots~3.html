private pure subroutine insert_knots(this, Xth, r, B, Bs)

Insert one or more knots without changing the represented curve.

Requests are applied in array order. r(i) copies of Xth(i) are added, and the resulting multiplicity may not exceed degree+1; multiplicity degree+1 represents a break. Let be the current active interval and the value returned by knot_tolerance. Writing for Xth(i), validation accepts , but inserts the supplied value exactly: it is not snapped to an endpoint and multiplicity uses exact stored-knot equality.

Rational curves are refined in homogeneous coordinates. Writing and for the old and new Euclidean control arrays, after the new weights are fixed the optional matrix Bs satisfies for every coordinate , and has shape [new_nc,old_nc]. B is the corresponding coordinate-block operator of shape [new_nc*ncoord,old_nc*ncoord] for control-point-major vectors. For a rational curve these operators therefore depend on both old and new weights; they are not the homogeneous refinement operator.

Algorithm: W. Boehm, "Inserting New Knots into B-Spline Curves," Computer-Aided Design 12 (1980), 199--201. doi:10.1016/0010-4485(80)90154-2. Implemented in the equivalent form of Piegl and Tiller, Algorithm A5.1.
