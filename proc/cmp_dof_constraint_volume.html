private pure subroutine cmp_dof_constraint_volume(this, rowptr, col, val, geometric)

Assemble sparse parametric and restricted geometric constraints for connected faces.

The one-based CSR arrays encode over unmerged, patch-concatenated control variables. For each mapped tangential face control, rows enforce normalized normal derivatives from order zero through the requested order. geometric optionally filters parametric (false) or geometric (true) connections. Geometric rows use only the scalar normal transition jet documented by forcad_multipatch. Rational projective scaling is included for compatible boundary weights. Apply the raw matrix to polynomial coefficients or homogeneous geometry columns. For a rational scalar field with coefficients , apply it to weighted numerator controls , not directly to .

For every mapped pair of tangential control indices and derivative order , one row discretizes

Parameter-range powers convert stored-knot derivatives to normalized normal derivatives. Call is_valid first; this routine assumes compatible connections. Coefficients with absolute value at most 128*epsilon(rk) are omitted from CSR storage, so the returned matrix is numerically sparsified rather than symbolically exact.
