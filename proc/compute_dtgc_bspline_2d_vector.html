private pure subroutine compute_dTgc_bspline_2d_vector(Xt, knot1, knot2, degree, nc, ng, dTgc, Tgc, wrap_parameters)

Evaluate polynomial surface basis values and gradients at many parameter pairs.

Outputs have shapes [ng_,ncp] and [ng_,ncp,2]. Every row contains at most (degree(1)+1)*(degree(2)+1) nonzero basis entries.
