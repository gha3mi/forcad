private impure subroutine export_Xth_in_Xg(this, filename, res, encoding)

Export active isoparametric grid lines embedded in physical space.

Every distinct active knot in one direction defines a line sampled over all active spans of the other direction. Optional res sets the minimum number of points per span and defaults to 10.
