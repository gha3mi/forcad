private pure subroutine rotate_Xc(this, alpha, beta, theta)

Rotate the control polygon using active extrinsic x-y-z Euler rotations. Angles alpha, beta, and theta are in degrees and apply . Cached physical samples are not changed. See rotation for the behavior of embeddings with fewer than three coordinates.
