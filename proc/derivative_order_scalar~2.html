private pure subroutine derivative_order_scalar(this, Xt, order, Dgc, elem)

Compute one requested mixed derivative at one surface parameter pair. Without elem, the result follows direction-1-fastest global control order. With elem, entries follow the supplied one-based flattened indices and an out-of-range index returns zero. A finite two-entry Xt is a caller precondition; this overload diagnoses its length and the derivative orders, but not nonfinite parameter values.
