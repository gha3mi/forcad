private pure subroutine set_ring(this, center, radius1, radius2)

Construct an exact planar rational annulus.

Three quadratic rational arcs form each circular boundary and a linear radial direction connects them. The result has a 7 x 2 control net in the xy plane at center(3). The two boundary radii are abs(radius1) and abs(radius2). For the named nondegenerate annulus, use nonzero scales of the same sign and unequal magnitudes (conventionally `0
