private pure function get_continuity(this) result(c)

Return for every distinct value in the stored knot vector. At an interior active knot this is the guaranteed continuity of the spline space; a particular curve can be smoother. Entries at active endpoints or outside the active interval describe representation multiplicity, not continuity between two active spans.
