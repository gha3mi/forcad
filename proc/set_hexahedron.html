private pure subroutine set_hexahedron(this, L, nc, Wc)

Construct an axis-aligned hexahedral tensor-product volume.

The control net is uniformly distributed on [0,L(1)] x [0,L(2)] x [0,L(3)]; direction 1 varies fastest. Generated knot vectors follow the standard set(nc=...,Xc=...) policy.
