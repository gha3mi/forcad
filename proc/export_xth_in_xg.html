private impure subroutine export_Xth_in_Xg(this, filename, res, encoding)

Export active isoparametric grid surfaces embedded in physical space.

Every distinct active knot in one direction defines a surface sampled over all active spans in the other directions. Optional res sets the minimum directional sampling density and defaults to 10.
