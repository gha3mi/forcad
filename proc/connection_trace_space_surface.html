private pure function connection_trace_space_surface(this, conn, tol) result(ok)

Test whether connected edges have compatible tangential spline spaces.

The mapped traces must have equal degree, equal control count, and affine-equivalent normalized knot vectors. Reversal of patch B is included in the comparison.
