private pure subroutine compute_d2Tgc_bspline_2d_scalar(Xt, knot1, knot2, degree, nc, d2Tgc, dTgc, Tgc, wrap_parameters)

Evaluate one dense polynomial surface basis, gradient, and Hessian.

Tgc has length ncp, dTgc shape [ncp,2], and the complete packed Hessian has shape [2*ncp,2]. Derivatives above a directional degree are zero.
