private pure subroutine cmp_dof_constraint_surface(this, rowptr, col, val, geometric)

Assemble sparse parametric and restricted geometric constraints for connected edges.

The one-based CSR arrays define over unmerged, patch-concatenated control variables. One row is emitted for every tangential boundary control and derivative order. geometric, when present, selects only parametric (false) or geometric (true) connections; when absent, both kinds are included. Geometric rows use only the scalar normal transition jet documented by forcad_multipatch. Rational projective scaling is incorporated for compatible boundary weights. Apply the raw matrix to polynomial coefficients or homogeneous geometry columns. For a rational scalar field with coefficients , apply it to weighted numerator controls , not directly to .

For every mapped tangential control and derivative order , one row discretizes

Tangential orientation is applied before row assembly. Parameter-range powers convert stored-knot derivatives to normalized normal derivatives. Call is_valid first; this routine assumes compatible connections. Coefficients with absolute value at most 128*epsilon(rk) are omitted from CSR storage, so the returned matrix is numerically sparsified rather than symbolically exact.
