private pure subroutine derivative_scalar(this, Xt, dTgc, Tgc, elem)

Evaluate the parametric basis gradient at one parameter pair. Optional elem selects and orders a local control subset. Xt must have at least two finite entries; later entries are ignored. The spline must be initialized. This convenience overload does not diagnose those preconditions.
