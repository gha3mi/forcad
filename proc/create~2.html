private pure subroutine create(this, res1, res2, Xt1, Xt2, Xt)

Sample and cache surface geometry.

Directional Xt1/Xt2 replace cached vectors; res1/res2 generate uniformly spaced vectors when the corresponding explicit vector is absent. Existing vectors are reused when neither is supplied. Unless explicit point rows Xt(:,1:2) are supplied, ndgrid(Xt1,Xt2) forms a direction-1-fastest Cartesian grid.
