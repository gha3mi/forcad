private pure subroutine set_C(this, center, radius1, radius2)

Construct an exact planar rational C-shaped annular sector.

Two quadratic rational arcs span 240 degrees on each circular boundary; a linear radial direction connects the boundaries. The result has a 5 x 2 control net in the xy plane at center(3). Boundary radii are the absolute scale values. Use nonzero scales of the same sign and unequal magnitudes for a regular C-shaped annular sector; opposite signs create crossing radial lines.
