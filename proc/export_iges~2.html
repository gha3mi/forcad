Export the current surface as IGES entity 128.

Both knot vectors, degrees, control-net ordering, weights, and active parameter ranges are transferred to a Rational B-Spline Surface entity. One- and two-dimensional embeddings are padded with zero coordinates; for embeddings above three dimensions, only the first three coordinates are written because entity 128 stores three Cartesian coordinates. Each directional wrapping option is written to both the corresponding IGES closed-direction and periodic-direction property. ForCAD does not independently verify control-net closure or periodic knot extension before setting those properties. The polynomial/rational property follows is_rational, including its roundoff-scale uniform-weight classification.

The routine requires initialized surface data. File-system and writer failures are delegated to forIGES and are not converted to err.

Format reference: U.S. National Bureau of Standards, Initial Graphics Exchange Specification (IGES), Version 4.0, NBSIR 88-3813 (1988), Rational B-Spline Surface Entity 128. NIST publication.
