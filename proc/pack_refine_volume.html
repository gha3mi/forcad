private pure subroutine pack_refine_volume(Xc, Wc, nc, dir, rational, Xdir)

Pack volume control lines for one-dimensional refinement.

The selected direction becomes the first dimension of Xdir; both orthogonal control directions and all physical coordinates are packed into columns. Rational input stores homogeneous values and a final weight component per orthogonal line. dir must be in 1:3 and Wc must be present when rational is true.
