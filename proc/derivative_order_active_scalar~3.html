private pure subroutine derivative_order_active_scalar(this, Xt, order, first_active, Dgc)

Compute one active arbitrary-order curve derivative. Entry Dgc(a+1) is the derivative of basis first_active+a, for a=0:degree. Wrapping and outside-domain behavior are identical to the vector overload.
