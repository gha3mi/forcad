private pure function cmp_dof_map_surface(this) result(map)

Build compact global identifiers for paired boundary controls. Higher normal-derivative conditions remain explicit in cmp_dof_constraint. The map trusts recorded topology and does not prove that paired controls are geometrically coincident; call is_valid first.

Disjoint-set implementation reference: R. E. Tarjan, JACM 22 (1975), 215--225. doi:10.1145/321879.321884.
