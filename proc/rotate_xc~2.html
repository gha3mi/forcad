private pure subroutine rotate_Xc(this, alpha, beta, theta)

Rotate all control points in place using x-y-z Euler angles in degrees.

The active extrinsic convention is . For fewer than three coordinates, only the leading principal matrix block is applied; coordinates after the third are unchanged. Cached physical samples are not changed.
