private impure subroutine export_Xth_in_Xg(this, filename, res, encoding)

Export knot-span parameter lines evaluated in physical space. Every nonzero active span receives at least res samples, with shared endpoints emitted once. The curve embedding must have two or three coordinates. encoding is forwarded to the legacy VTK writer.
