private pure function compute_Tgc_nurbs_3d_scalar(Xt, knot1, knot2, knot3, degree, nc, Wc, elem, wrap_parameters) result(Tgc)

Evaluate the rational volume basis at one parameter triple.

Dense output has length product(nc). With elem, result order follows the supplied flattened global indices and Wc may be global or local; inactive and invalid listed controls remain zero.
