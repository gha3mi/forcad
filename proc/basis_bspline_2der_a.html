private pure subroutine basis_bspline_2der_A(Xt, knot, nc, degree, d2B, dB, B)

Evaluate dense B-spline values and derivatives through second order.

Each output has length nc; unsupported derivatives, including all second derivatives when degree<2, are returned as zero.
