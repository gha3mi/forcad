public pure function kron_eye(A, ncoord) result(B)

Expand a scalar operator independently over ncoord components.

The result is the control-point-major representation of : B((i-1)*ncoord+r,(j-1)*ncoord+r)=A(i,j). Nonpositive ncoord returns B(0,0).
