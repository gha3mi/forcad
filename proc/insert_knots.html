private pure subroutine insert_knots(this, dir, Xth, r, B, Bs)

Insert knots in one tensor direction without changing the volume. Requests are applied in array order. The resulting multiplicity may not exceed degree(dir)+1; equality represents a break in the selected direction. Let be its current active interval and the value returned by knot_tolerance. Writing for Xth(i), validation accepts , but inserts the supplied value exactly: no endpoint snapping or tolerance-based multiplicity matching is applied.

Rational data are refined in homogeneous coordinates. Writing and for the old and new Euclidean control arrays, after the new weights are fixed Bs[new_control,old_control] is the full flattened tensor-product operator satisfying for every coordinate . B is its coordinate-block expansion for control-point-major vectors. For rational data these are Euclidean fixed-weight maps that depend on the old and new weights, not homogeneous refinement maps.

Algorithm: W. Boehm, Computer-Aided Design 12 (1980), 199--201. doi:10.1016/0010-4485(80)90154-2. Applied directionally using Piegl and Tiller, Algorithm A5.1.
