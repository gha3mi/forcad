type, public :: nurbs_multipatch_surface

Ordered collection of connected NURBS surface patches.

Valid side labels are u_min, u_max, v_min, and v_max, with the documented aliases accepted by connect. reverse reverses the single tangential boundary coordinate. continuity=-1 records topology without constraints; order zero merges matching boundary controls.

Diagnostic codes are: 201 invalid patch identifier; 202 invalid side; 203 invalid continuity metadata; 204 incompatible interface geometry or trace space; and 205 invalid patch state.

    Author
    Seyed Ali Ghasemi
    License
    BSD 3-Clause

    Record two surface edges with parametric or restricted geometric metadata. geometric defaults to .false. for C^n continuity. For G^n, reparameterization stores the arbitrary-order transition jet between normalized coordinates normal to the connected sides. Tangential orientation is controlled independently by reverse. The geometric map is restricted to ; no transition derivative may vary along the edge or couple normal motion into the tangential coordinate.
