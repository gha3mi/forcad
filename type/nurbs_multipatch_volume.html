type, public :: nurbs_multipatch_volume

Ordered collection of connected trivariate NURBS patches.

Valid side labels are u_min/u_max, v_min/v_max, and w_min/w_max, including the short aliases accepted by connect. The patch-B face map can reverse orientation, swap its two tangential axes, and flip either mapped axis. continuity=-1 stores topology without constraints.

Diagnostic codes are: 201 invalid patch identifier; 202 invalid side; 203 invalid continuity metadata; 204 incompatible interface geometry or trace space; and 205 invalid patch state.

  • private pure subroutine connect_volume(this, patch_a, side_a, patch_b, side_b, continuity, reverse, swap, flip, geometric, reparameterization)

    Author
    Seyed Ali Ghasemi
    License
    BSD 3-Clause

    Record two volume faces with parametric or restricted geometric metadata. geometric defaults to .false. for C^n continuity. For G^n, reparameterization stores the arbitrary-order transition jet between normalized coordinates normal to the connected faces. Existing reverse/swap/flip metadata defines the tangential face map. The geometric model is restricted to : its normal jet is constant over the face and normal motion does not alter tangential coordinates.
