Trivariate tensor-product B-spline and NURBS volumes.

For degrees , , and , the rational volume mapping is

Control subscripts in this module's formulas are one-based to match Fortran storage. They are equivalent to conventional zero-based tensor indices after subtracting one in each direction.

Let and . The rational tensor basis is nonnegative and forms a partition of unity on the active domain because the weights are positive. Every positive-order mixed derivative that exists therefore sums to zero over all basis functions. Polynomial tensor-basis derivatives vanish when an order exceeds the degree in its direction, but rational quotient derivatives generally need not.

Direction 1 varies fastest in every flattened tensor array: linear(i,j,k)=i+nc(1)*((j-1)+nc(2)*(k-1)). Omitting weights, or using equal weights, selects the polynomial tensor-product B-spline form. More precisely, supplied weights are classified as uniform when their maximum deviation from the first weight is at most 32*epsilon(rk)*maxval(Wc); that path treats them as exactly equal and omits rational normalization.

Every direction accepts any finite, nondecreasing knot vector satisfying the spline-space invariants, including uniform or nonuniform, clamped, one-sided clamped, unclamped, and periodic-form vectors. This is structural: periodic knot-spacing extension and control-lattice closure are supplied by the caller, not inferred from the knot arrays. The active interval in direction d is [knot_d(degree(d)+1),knot_d(nc(d)+1)]. Parameter wrapping is only an evaluation policy and does not create periodic geometry. At an interior knot of multiplicity , the spline space has guaranteed directional continuity , although special control data can be smoother. At a discontinuous derivative, evaluation follows the selected one-sided span convention.

Directional refinement applies an exact one-dimensional operator to every homogeneous control line, preserving to working precision on the active domain. Positive degree elevation converts a non-clamped directional representation to an equivalent active-domain open-clamped representation and does not retain periodic seam constraints. nurbs_volume supports evaluation, mixed basis derivatives, IGA element and face data, directional refinement, volume quadrature, fitting, discrete and local continuous nearest-point searches, transformations, and legacy VTK export. IGES has no implemented trivariate export path in this API; export_iges reports that condition through err.

private interface compute_d2Tgc

Evaluate dense parametric Hessians of the volume basis.

For ncp=product(nc), scalar output uses shape [3*ncp,3] and stores at d2Tgc((a-1)*ncp+A,b). Vector output prepends the parameter-point dimension. All mixed entries are represented explicitly. This implementation generic is private; use derivative_order when only one derivative multi-index is required.
